Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how talesbyoguz (the publishing name of Oğuz Kılınç, an individual developer) handles information in the iOS application Wakeboy - Loud Alarm Clock (also referred to as Angry Alarm) and related mobile applications published by talesbyoguz.

1. Summary — No Personal Data Collected

We do not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share your personal data. Angry Alarm does not require a user account and does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your alarms, app preferences, and mission completion records (including text you write during typing missions for your in-app Reports) are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's local storage frameworks (SwiftData and UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device to our servers.

3. Device Permissions

Angry Alarm requests only the permissions required for its alarm and mission features:

  • Alarms (AlarmKit): Used solely to schedule, display, and sound the alarms you create.
  • Notifications: Used solely for local on-device reminders (such as trial expiration reminders and mission return notices). We do not operate remote push notification servers.
  • Motion & Fitness (CoreMotion): Used solely on-device to count steps or read phone movement during motion-based wake-up missions. Motion data is processed in real time on your device and is never stored externally or transmitted.

4. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed exclusively by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not collect or have access to your payment method, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. Your purchases are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Crash Reports, Diagnostics, and TestFlight Feedback

If you opt in to share analytics or crash diagnostics with app developers in your iOS device settings, or if you submit beta feedback or screenshots through Apple's TestFlight app, Apple processes and provides that diagnostic information to us under Apple's Privacy Policy. We use it solely to diagnose crashes and fix bugs.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because app data is stored locally on your device, deleting the app removes its local data from your device. If you have iCloud Backup or encrypted device backups enabled in iOS, Apple may retain a backup copy until it is overwritten or deleted according to your Apple backup settings.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

8. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change (for example, if we add new services in a future version), we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above before those changes take effect.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at talesbyoguz@gmail.com.
