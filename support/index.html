App Support

Wakeboy - Loud Alarm Clock (Angry Alarm) & apps by talesbyoguz (Oğuz Kılınç)

Need help, found a bug, or have feedback?

Email us directly at talesbyoguz@gmail.com. When reporting an issue, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

Common Questions

  • Alarms not ringing: Open iOS Settings → Apps → Angry Alarm and make sure Alarms and Notifications permissions are enabled.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription: Subscriptions are managed directly by Apple. Open iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions (or tap Manage subscription inside Angry Alarm Settings).
  • Restore purchases: Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
